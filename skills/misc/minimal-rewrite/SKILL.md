## Task

Rewrite the selected text with minimal changes. Check for grammar mistakes, awkward phrasing, or incoherent structure.

## Goal

- Resulting text should be highly readable for assumed reader with proper tone.
- Resulting text does not lose its originality.

## Rule

- Do not add any additional em-dashes except necessary
- Do not add any additional semicolon except necessary
